# AdhikarAI ⚖️🤖

> **Empowering Citizens with Smart, Accessible Legal Intelligence and Rights Management.**

AdhikarAI is an advanced, AI-driven legal ecosystem designed to simplify Indian law, bridge the gap between citizens and government administration, and provide instant, accurate legal assistance. By removing complex legal jargon, AdhikarAI ensures that fundamental rights, government welfare, and administrative paperwork are accessible to everyone.

---

## 🌟 Core Features

### 1. 📝 RTI Draft Agent
Filing a Right to Information (RTI) application can often be intimidating due to structural and departmental complexities. 
* **Smart Wizard:** Seamlessly guides users through selecting their specific grievance category (e.g., Passport, Municipal Roads, Police, Ration Cards).
* **Automated Drafting:** Generates legally sound, precise, and professional RTI application drafts customized for the target Public Information Officer (PIO).
* **Portal & Offline Support:** Offers direct routing to official government central/state RTI portals or exports ready-to-print professional PDF application formats.

### 2. 🏛️ Schemes Navigator
Navigating thousands of central and state welfare schemes is effortless with the Schemes Navigator.
* **Personalized Discovery:** Matches users with government schemes, subsidies, and financial aid programs based on their eligibility profile.
* **Benefit Breakdown:** Translates complex eligibility criteria, required documentation, and application deadlines into simple, easy-to-understand terms.
* **Direct Action Links:** Provides step-by-step roadmaps and direct pathways to apply for targeted welfare benefits.

### 3. 🔍 Rights Navigator
The Rights Navigator acts as an interactive compendium for constitutional, statutory, and everyday legal rights.
* **Real-Life Scenarios:** Explores practical situations (e.g., traffic stops, police questioning, consumer protection, and tenant rights) through a clear "Myth vs. Fact" framework.
* **Act & Section Citations:** Every answer references specific legal frameworks, including the Constitution of India, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), and specialized civil/criminal statutes.
* **Gamified Legal Literacy:** Features interactive modules allowing users to track progress, earn milestones, and elevate their understanding of civic rights.

### 4. 📄 FormFiller
Administrative paperwork and legal documentation are streamlined using intelligent automation.
* **Guided Data Collection:** Prompts users with simple, conversational questions to gather necessary details for legal or administrative forms.
* **Auto-Population:** Automatically populates official document templates, affidavits, complaints, and applications accurately.
* **Error Prevention:** Performs automated syntax and completeness checks to drastically minimize rejections or administrative delays.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** React.js / Progressive Web App (PWA) framework optimized for rapid mobile and web navigation.
* **Backend:** Asynchronous Python backend powered by FastAPI for lightning-fast request processing.
* **AI & Natural Language Processing:** Context-aware language models fine-tuned on Indian statutory frameworks, case laws, and administrative procedures.
* **Database & Storage:** Secure relational data management ensuring strict user privacy and document security.
